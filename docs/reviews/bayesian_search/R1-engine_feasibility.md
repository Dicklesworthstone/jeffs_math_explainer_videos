## How to build each recurring device

**(a) The dots.**
- Every dot is a `Dot` with a flat fill and no stroke. A `Dot` keeps its circle tag, so the renderer fills it with its analytic disc kernel (the spike's `GlyphKind::Dots`). Never `DotCloud` or `glow_dots`: the retained route refuses them (`vector_frame.rs`), and they would move the chapter to the slow camera route.
- Keep each field as a struct of handles: the dots, each dot's cell, and one shared `Rc<RefCell<Vec<bool>>>` saying which are lit. Do not rely on the group (E14).
- **Going out and staying in place:** one updater on a controller mobject, as `netfuls.rs` and `cheating.rs` do. As the strip's leading edge passes a dot the search would have found, it writes that dot's fill once and clears its flag. Placement never changes.
- **Percentages:** closures that count the flags, through a readout that rounds first (E5). The picture and the number then come from one list.
- **100 dots:** each its own `Dot`, plus a teal ring per dot kept at opacity 0 until "would be found" is shown.
- **2,000 dots:** `props::Swarm::build`, then your own dt updater that shifts each member (E8).
- **10,000 dots:** S-4's flight, which Video A's 04_5 ships (E7).
- **75,000:** draw 3,000 and say so (E1).

**(b) The 1968 map.** One crate prop built at a stated size, never scaled (E4): one `RowStrips::build` of 16 strips by 20 cells; 38 two-point lattice lines on top; the 172 numbers as `kit.text` from `y1968::POINTS`, optional; the letters and row numbers. Recolour with `RowStrips::recolor` from an updater, only when something changes. For the model search, precompute `model_search(rule, 2/3, 81)` into per-search colours and drive both maps from one tracker (E4).

**(c) The countdown.** `kit.sonify` with the ticks as a WAV, one linear tracker tween from N to 0, the ring as an arc that follows the tracker, the numeral as the tracker's ceiling (E3).

**(d) The fan.** The 950 distinct segments of all 65,536 orders, as thin flat quads, revealed one search at a time while the counter reads 4, 16, 64, up to 65,536 (E2).

**(e) The lanes.** One flat opaque rectangle per lane, shown by one updater that also advances a shared lane count; the readout looks that count up in a table precomputed by `koopman::swept_after` (E10).

**(f) Lower-thirds and the code panel.** `props::lower_third` and `props::CodePanel` as they are, with the limits in E12 and E15.

**(g) TeX.** The sources and their status are in E11.
