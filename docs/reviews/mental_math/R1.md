**Blocker:** one, raised twice (R1, D1). A programmers' lower-third said that
stopping after any piece leaves "the digits that matter most already right".

test now pins 19 × 19 so the claim cannot come back.

**What changed overall.**

- **One move, drawn one way** (M1, D2, R2, R3, R9, C1, C2). Four reviewers
  found that the ending asked the viewer to recall a strip moving in the 48
  picture, where no strip had moved. Chapters 02, 08, 10 and 14 are restaged
  round a single gesture, which is also the order's own picture: a strip is
  sliced off the side of a square and laid along its top, leaving a
  rectangle and a small square that sticks out. 35² is 30 by 40 and a
  corner; 48² is 46 by 50 and a corner, and 46 fifties is Bethe's 2300;
  47 × 53 is the same move run backwards. What this costs: the earlier
  pictures' two best lines, "the two fives make one more ten" and the corner
  "taken off twice", are gone (D26, D27, R27, R28, M27, M29 liked them).
  Their replacements are "five narrower, five taller: thirty-five sits
  halfway between thirty and forty" and "the end of the strip sticks out".
- **The order of the acts** (C3). The everyday tricks now come after parts
  and before the middle and near fifty, so the story and its line lead
  straight into the mixed round and the ending. The owner fixed only the
  first two tricks' order; the brief marks this as a default he can reverse.
- **The quiz device** (C4, C13, C19, M2). At zero the answer lands alone, on
  the countdown's last note, and is then worked; the first quiz after each
  trick replays its picture; the cold open's card has no pause line; three
  kinds of question have three looks.
- **Colours** (R8, D6, E3, C9). Three role pairs were under the storyboard's
  own bar (14.9, 17.3, 19.6). The strips are now green `#70CCA1` and the
  corner orange `#CC811F`; gold is an outline on the moving piece, never a
  fill; the corpus green is not used. Least pair 24.2.
- **Limits said where the trick is taught** (D3, D4, D9, D11, D12): when
  halving pays, when round then fix pays, what the middle needs, what an
  estimate catches.
- **Set pieces** (C5, C6, C7): the sweep over every number in front (03_5),
  the pour (05_2), the machine that is still running after Bethe has
  answered (09_3), the live square of fifty whose corner overflows at 62
  (11_5), the six tiles that are the six pictures (12).
- **Length.** The additions took the estimate to 17.9 min. To pay for them
  six quizzes were cut (65², 57², 54², 65 × 75, 4% of 75, 5 × 64), with the
  times-nine beat and the spoken beat about the 1844 school book. There are
  now 17 countdown quizzes and six open questions, at least two countdown
  quizzes for each trick counting the mixed round. Estimate 15.7 min, every chapter
  at most 77 s.
