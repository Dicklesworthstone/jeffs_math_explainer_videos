## Straight answers to the questions put to this lane

- **Does the arc hold for 15 minutes with 22 quizzes?** It holds to 09_8, about ten minutes in: each trick is stated, questioned, drawn, tried and given its limit, and the story sits at the midpoint. It does not hold from there to chapter 14. Half the quizzes and the thinnest pictures fall in the four minutes after the video has stated its thesis (C3, C4).
- **The "your turn" device.** It is the right device and the cold open teaches it in about 13 seconds. As specified it is monotonous: one cycle 22 times, 15 answers in type, and a cadence that resolves before the answer shows. Where it should vary is set out in C4 (the answer on the last tick, three answer modes, the worked picture as a crib for the first try), C6 (tiles that are pictures, one quiz that lights two of them), C8 (the landing chord, one countdown that ends in silence), C18 and C19 (a last quiz the viewer wins, ring length).
- **Do the "why" pictures carry the idea?** Yes for the fives (02), parts (04), the running total (05), the middle (06), and times 5, halve and double, times 9 and prices (11). The two near-fifty pictures each carry their sum but neither is the move of chapter 02 (C1). The percent swap's picture carries "8 × 25 is 25 × 8" and not "per hundred" (C12).
- **Is the strip picture continuous across chapters?** No. Five gestures share a name and a colour, the 48 picture has no moving strip at all, and the treatment is described two ways (C1, C2). The colour that marks the move is the closest pair in the palette to the corner it passes (C9).
- **Where does the energy sag?** From 08_5 to the end of 09 (about 86 s of type) and from 11_5 to 13_1 (about two minutes, nine quizzes, no figure). The second is the serious one (C3 to C6).
- **Feynman and Bethe.** Placed well and sourced carefully; told to the ear (C10).
- **Is the ending earned?** The idea of it is. The pictures under it are not yet (C1, C11).
- **Slideshow of equations.** 03_1 to 03_5 and 03_7; 07_6; 08_5 and 08_6; all of 09; 10_6, 10_7, 10_9 and 10_10; 11_5 to 11_10; all of 12. (02_1 to 02_3 are also type, and acceptable: twenty seconds of rule before the question that the picture answers.)
- **On screen for the ear only.** Three props and three silent lower-thirds (C21, C15).
- **The owner's words.** Served: the centrepiece with its why; 23 × 46 by parts with the zeros put back by size; "the hardest part is remembering the 4 numbers" as beats of its own; (a + b)(a − b); near fifty with the story; four more tricks that pass his three tests; many quizzes. Underserved: "some time to think about it" (C19); the "most useful and practical" tricks sit in the trough (C3); the anecdote's telling (C10); and the corpus-wide ask for spectacle (C7).
- **The rule of 72.** I agree with the cut (C20).
