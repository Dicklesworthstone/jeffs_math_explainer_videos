## Comprehension check: the key picture of each chapter

- **01_eighty_five_squared.** A violet quiz card whose ring drains for four seconds, the answer 8 × 9 = 72 then 25 built where the ring was, and the paper sum's rows overflowing three slots "in the head": feasible on the retained route from a crate-local quiz card, per-digit text and primitives, once the countdown has a mechanism (E1) and the head has a drawable stand-in (E18).
- **02_ends_in_five.** A 35 by 35 square on a faint grid, cut at 30 into a blue block, two teal strips and an amber corner, whose top strip turns a quarter turn to sit beside the right strip, after which the 30 by 40 block is ruled into twelve hundreds and counted: feasible as five flat rectangles moved by placement over line groups (E7, E8, E9), in colours that need to change (E3).

- **05_running_total.** One large running total beside the cut rectangle running 800, 920, 1040, 1058 as each piece pours in, then a 0 to 1,200 number line: feasible with a tracker readout the kit does not yet have in the needed form (E4) and a hand-built line (E15).
- **06_the_middle.** The 47 by 53 rectangle laid over a 50 by 50 outline, its 3-wide overhang turning into the 3-tall gap and leaving a 3 by 3 red hole: feasible by placement (E7), but the 3 is 70 px of a 4K frame at true scale (E2), and the chapter is 2 s under the cap (E21).
- **07_bethe.** Two name cards, an outline desk calculator whose dials stop on 2304, and Bethe's question on a quote card: feasible from primitives and Text, with stacked numerals for the dials (E17) and the existing quote-card pattern (E19); the 48-in-50 square at 07_5 shares E2.
- **08_near_fifty.** The 48 square inside the 50 square with two red 50 by 2 strips crossing in a 2 by 2 corner, then the 53 square's two strips laid end to end as 100 by 3: buildable from rectangles, but not legible, and at one point not on the frame, at true scale as described (E2).
- **09_your_turn_fifty.** Three quiz cards answered as two digit pairs (27 04), 62² with the 1 of 144 carrying into the hundreds, and Feynman's sentence on a card: feasible; the pairs and the carry are glyph-level moves (E10), and the quote's dash and marks are in the bundled faces (E14, E19).
- **10_percents.** The $48.00 bill as a bar of ten strips with one lifted out and the decimal point hopping, then the 8 by 25 rectangle turned on its side: feasible with a new bar builder and fixed-pitch digits (E16) and escaped percent and dollar signs (E12).

- **13_nothing_to_grab.** The 67 by 78 rectangle in four pieces with a running total to 5226, paper and a calculator sliding in, and two wrong readings crossed in red beside the estimate 5600: feasible as written with the cut rectangle, the total of E4 and drawn crosses (E17).
- **14_near_something_you_know.** The three strip pictures side by side, each moving its strip once more, three numeric identities giving way to a² = (a − d)(a + d) + d², Chase's page as a card, and a lit number line with the receipts: feasible, with the small-picture scale problem (E2), a three-into-one morph that must be designed (E5) and dissolving groups (E11).
