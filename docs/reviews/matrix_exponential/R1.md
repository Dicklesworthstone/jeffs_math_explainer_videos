# R1 dispositions — matrix_exponential (Video J)

Round R1 on the storyboard (plan §5.2), 2026-10-13, against the storyboard at
commit `767fd6c`. Reviews (verbatim, machine paths made repository-relative):
`R1-maya_14.md` (M), `R1-dev_28.md` (D), `R1-ruth_45.md` (R),
`R1-engine_feasibility.md` (E), `R1-creative_director.md` (C). 175 findings:
2 blockers, 57 majors, 90 minors, 26 delights.

Dispositions: **fixed** (in `explainer_corpus/matrix_exponential/docs/storyboard.md`,
`docs/fact-sheet.md`, `docs/brief.md` or the facts; beat ids are the revised
ones), **answered** (why not, one sentence), or **deferred** (to a bead:
`.5` is the script, lexicon and `docs/description.md`; `.6` the build; `.7`
the drafts; `.8` narration and score, filed with this round). Gate G2r: no
finding without a disposition; no open blocker.

**Blockers:** two, both fixed.

- **D1 (with M1, R1): the hopper quiz did not say which hopper.** For the
  natural reading, one hop per tick, the busy node is the right answer
  (3/8); "none of them" is right only for a hopper that leaves along each
  link at rate one. The fact sheet claimed the quiz named it; the card did
  not. The quiz is cut. 12_3 is one relabel beat, and its strip names the
  hopper and gives both answers (`F-D.chance.per_tick` is new). Fact sheet
  finding 8 is corrected.
- **C1: over the owner's 17 minutes** (17:28 by the gate, before repairs
  that cost more). The cold-open ring, the hopper quiz and the
  four-dimension count are gone; chapter 13 is five beats; three pairs of
  beats are merged; about forty lines are tighter. The gate now reads
  17.3 min, 16.9 without the 2 s it adds on top of each countdown. The
  first drafts time the beats whose animation outruns its words.

**What changed overall.** Five things that more than one reviewer found:

1. **The loops were multiplied in the wrong order** (D6, R9, C5). The
   facts and the oracle shared the order, so facts-check could not see it;
   the picture would have turned one way and the equation said the other.
   Both loops (the square's, the cube's) are now multiplied in the order
   they are played, both leave minus the commutator, a test says so, and
   the cube's loop is repeated twenty times so that it can be seen
   (45.7°, `F-L.creep.*`).
2. **The rule that "would break" was pictured as the rule that never
   does** (R2, M16, M3, C10): one time axis split in two is one rule run
   for two durations. The rule is now stated where it is needed, at 14_1,
   as two shares at once against one after the other; 09_6 keeps "one
   rule, run twice" apart from it; the nine-minute corner box is gone.
3. **That the series is the flow was asserted** (R4, D5, C3, M15). 09_4
   replays the row slide of 04_6 with a matrix in it: every term slides one
   place and leaves one A in front, so the rate of e^{tA} is A times
   itself.
4. **"Rule" named two readings of a matrix** (R5, M17, M19, C2). A matrix
   is a machine; used as a move its output is where you land, used as a
   rule its output is your velocity; the exponential takes a rule and
   returns a move. "Rate equals amount" (never "size"); "share" for r;
   "spin" for the rule and "rotation" for the move.
5. **The ending restated the opening** (C4, M20, M9, D19, R14). The reason
   for one radian is now planted at 06_4 (speed equals distance from the
   centre, so a time t is an arc of length t), the opening question returns
   as the last quiz, and its picture is the exact bridge for this example:
   a line of tiny steps wrapped onto a circle of rotations.

Also new from this round: a second family of edge cases (thirteen named
degenerate matrices judged in exact fractions, R22); a matrix whose terms
sit strictly under their ceilings (M22, E6); the 400-node network (C7); the
64-bit and too-many-halvings runs (D3, D4, R13); the tanks as a dot on the
flow's plane (C8). Facts: 353 keys, `facts-check` PASS; 25 tests.
