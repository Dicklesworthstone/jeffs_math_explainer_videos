## Key picture per chapter

- **01_e_to_a_matrix.** A violet quiz card with e raised to a 2 by 2 grid and a ring draining from 5, answered by a grid of four decimals beside a dot plane turning one radian: buildable once the quiz card has a mechanism (E1) and the grid is one prop that can sit in an exponent (E2); the dots turn cheaply.
- **02_the_obvious_guess.** The identity leaves a dot plane still and the matrix of four ones slides every dot onto the diagonal: buildable if the dots are moved one by one and the plane is small enough that the squashed image stays in the frame (E15).
- **03_growth.** Compounding staircases settle into a smooth curve on which a point carries a slope triangle and two equal live readouts: buildable as written from a data window, polylines and two live numbers (E29 for a cheap improvement).
- **04_the_series.** The triangle under y = x grows at the height of its right edge, then the finished row "slides one place over onto itself": the triangle is placement-only and buildable; the slide is not buildable with `TransformMatchingTex` as written and needs explicit pairs (E5).
- **05_one_function.** A running total ticks to 2.718 beside the bank's number, the staircase and the partial sums close on one curve, and the product rule goes into a gold box: buildable; the total must step, not tween (E12), and the box does not survive the chapter (E17).
- **06_turning.** A quarter turn on the number plane, straight steps drifting off the circle, and a gold point travelling the upper half circle to minus one: buildable as written from a data-window grid, polylines and one tracker (E22).
- **07_a_matrix_in.** Every x becomes A, J's powers go round a dial, the terms drop into two piles that become cosine and sine, and the plane turns under four live entries: buildable; 07_2 works as cross-fades, 07_5 needs per-term pieces with explicit pairs (E11).
- **08_does_it_settle.** Term bars rise to 26 and collapse while the running total swings out to radius 21.5 and comes home to the unit circle, then the bars sit under outline bars: buildable only with a zoom of about fifteen times for the homecoming (E7), and the outline-bar picture contradicts the facts for 5J (E6).
- **09_follow_the_arrows.** About 80 dots, each with a teal velocity arrow, flow for a time t leaving gold trails: buildable at roughly 6 to 10 fps at 4K (my estimate) if the trails are precomputed curves revealed from the tracker, as Video C did, and not `TracedPath`; it needs a probe (E8).
- **10_two_tanks.** Two tanks whose amber follows the model, two equations whose numbers lift into a grid, a table of step counts and two curves closing on 50: buildable as written from primitives (E11 for the lift).
- **11_walks.** A four-node network beside its grid of ones and zeros, walks traced on it, and e^A as a shaded grid: buildable as written; an out-and-back walk needs offset arcs or a moving token (E23).
- **12_heat_and_chance.** Node glow and four bars follow e^{−tL}, a hopper jumps, and two bars settle while two others slosh for ever: buildable by recolouring in place and rebuilding bars; there is no bloom, and anything that reaches exactly zero must not be scaled by placement (E14, E20).
- **13_how_computers_do_it.** Bars climb to 9.3 million above four numbers smaller than two, a red wrong grid stands beside the true one, and a halve-then-square chain turns it green: buildable from static bars and grids (E21).
- **14_order_matters.** The gold box returns, one square becomes three different parallelograms, and AB − BA lands as the commutator: buildable; the "both at once" morph should be driven from the model so it is the true flow (E16).
- **15_the_doorway.** Skew grids, a cube turned by e^{t·spin}, a four-move loop that leaves a small spin, and a line on a flat sheet carried onto a curve on a surface: buildable on the retained route by projection as Videos C and E did, but the leftover spin is invisible at the fact sheet's angles (E9) and the curved surface has no stated construction (E10).
- **16_where_it_leads.** An arm tagged with one exponential per joint, twelve tiles filling as 8 + 3 + 1, a recap strip and the opening card answered: buildable; W⁺ and W⁻ must be TeX (E13) and the receipts card needs wrapped lines and time to be read (E24).

## Cost of the heavy beats (my estimates)

- **Dot planes (01_2, 02_4, 02_5, 07_6, 16_5):** about 120 dots, one shift each per frame while the tracker moves, plus at most four live numbers. Scaled from S-2 (1,000 two-part fish at about 700 CPU-ms a frame at 4K): 40 to 85 ms a frame, at or near this Mac's 15 fps encoder bound. S-8 measured 18 live numbers and two recoloured maps at 36 to 46 ms with no growth over 80 s, so the readouts are safe.
- **09_2 and 09_3:** 80 dots, 80 arrows (two parts each), 24 trails cut back per frame: about 100 to 170 ms a frame before trails, 6 to 10 fps at 4K60. With 80 `TracedPath` trails, expect several times worse; that is what the probe in E8 is for.
- **15 and 16_1 by projection:** a cube is 6 faces or 12 edges rewritten only while a tracker moves; a dotted sphere is about 150 shifts a frame. Both are below the dot planes.
- **Quiz cards:** one arc and one digit rebuilt per frame for 58 s in all.
- **Whole video:** 17.5 min is 63,000 frames at 60 fps; at about 15 fps that is about 70 minutes for one process, less with chapters side by side. No chapter approaches the raised raw-frame cap.
