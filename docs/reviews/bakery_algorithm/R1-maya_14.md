**What I checked, and how** (in my head or on paper):

- **01_5:** 41 + 1 = 42 twice, so one point is lost against 43.
- **01_6–01_7:** I listed all six staircases on a 2×2 grid (RRUU, RURU, RUUR, URRU, URUR, UURR). The four through the middle are all except RRUU and UURR.
- **03_4–03_5:** max(0, 3, 0, 2) + 1 = 4. With slates 0, 3, 0, 4, 2 the serving order is 5 → 2 → 4.
- **05_1–05_3:** I walked the trace. Both see 0, both pick 1. Customer 2 sees 0 and walks in. Customer 1 writes 1 and wins the tie on name.
- **04_5:** (2,4) comes before (3,1), because 2 < 3.
- **06_3–06_4:** customer 4 sees 1, 1 and takes 2. Customer 1 sees 1, 1, 2 and takes 3. Customer 2 comes back, sees 3 as the biggest and takes 4. The order (1,2), (1,3), (2,4), (3,1), (4,2) is column by column.
- **08_1:** writing 09 → 10 one digit at a time gives 19 (tens digit first) or 00 (ones digit first).
- **08_5:** 7 + 1 = 8.
- **09_4** (on paper, with a calculator): 1000 × 86,400 × 365.25 = 31,557,600,000, and 2³⁵ = 34,359,738,368, so the first is smaller.
