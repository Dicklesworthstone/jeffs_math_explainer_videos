Reviewer: creative director (`tools/review_prompts/creative_director.md`). Stage: R1, storyboard.

## Verdict

The spine is right and the hero picture is the right one: five cubes that cut into slabs and close into a 15 by 15 square,
previewed in 23 s, built slab by slab in 05, re-read as thin odd layers in 06, returned at 15. The video's honesty
device (the card, failing once) is good. Most of the pictures carry their claim silent (03, 04, 05, 08, 10, 11, 12,
13, and the vanish in 14). One does not: the reason every cube fits (06_3) is an algebra card. One is partly
invisible: the three-stack lock (07) sits in the interior of a block seen from one side. One number is hidden by the
drawing: the 31st region of the circle (02_6) is a speck. And the sound plan, as written, would fail its own hard
rules and spend its one resolution nine times before 15_6.

No blocker: every chapter has one key picture, and no picture I could check is false. 11 major, 17 minor, 5 delight
(33 findings; counts at the end). The majors are all fixable inside the storyboard.
