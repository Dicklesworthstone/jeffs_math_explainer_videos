# R1 review: engine feasibility, visual_proofs (Proofs You Can See)

Reviewer role: engine feasibility (`tools/review_prompts/engine_feasibility.md`).
Pin: franken_manim `a399d626c43ecc852a5c795288979f52b446bc5c` (`FRANKEN_MANIM.pin`).
Nothing was compiled or run (no cargo on this machine): every cost below is an estimate from reading code and from the plan's spike numbers, and says so.
`<scratch>` is `R1-engine_feasibility/` beside this file (`counts.py` makes the face and piece counts; `fmn-src/` is `git archive` of franken_manim `crates/` at the pin, read only).

## 1. Bottom line

- No blocker. Every picture the storyboard asks for can be drawn at the pin on the retained 2D route with a fixed projection; no beat needs the camera route.
- The cube method is **unproven at the pin for solids that re-sort while they move**, and the brief's "bakery chapter 06 proved the method" is an overstatement (E1). That is the one real schedule risk: chapters 01, 07, 08, 09 and 12 stand on it. Gate them on a one-day spike (S-10 below).
- Two picture promises are not literal as written and need a change: 04_2's "half turn" (E3) and 14_5's "stretches tenfold upward" (E4).
- One design default of the paused builder (a piece of many tiles as ONE mobject) defeats the renderer's interior fast path and should not be adopted for the 15 by 15 and 21 by 21 boards (E2).
- The sound plan's "each piece lands as one note of the chord" cannot be done with score marks (E5).
- Render budget: about 56,200 frames at 4K60 (937 s, G2 estimate). By estimate no cube chapter is slower than Hoeffding's 15 fps reference once E2 is followed; light chapters are x264-bound.

Counts by severity: 0 blocker, 6 major, 10 minor, 3 delight (19 findings).
