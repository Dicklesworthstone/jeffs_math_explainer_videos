Dispositions: **fixed** (in `explainer_corpus/visual_proofs/docs/storyboard.md`
unless another file is named; beat ids are the revised ones), **kept**

**What the reviewers checked.** Maya, Dev, Ruth and the auditor each rebuilt
the pictures and recomputed the numbers with their own scripts (exact
integers and fractions): every number the first draft said or showed was
right. What they found wrong was in what the words claim, what the cards leave
out, and what the pictures could not show from one viewpoint. The engine
reviewer read the code at the pin.

**What changed overall.**

- **The first proof's card no longer says "we checked that"** (R1, M4, D4).
  03_3 gives the geometry, 03_4 says the arm is as long as the side it wraps,
  and 03_5 pulls back to the hundredth layer (99 + 99 + 1 = 199). Chapter 02
  takes apart exactly the move the first card had made.
- **Each proof is given its reason as a picture** (the idea passes). 06_3 is
  the arm's pieces paired (the earlier cubes' sides laid end to end: 1 + 5,
  2 + 4, the 3 alone, the half slab found); 07_4 takes the locked block apart
  slab by slab and hands the L from blue to the other two; 08_2 shows the
  empty half is chapter 04's second staircase; 09_2 shows every slab gaining
  an L and one slab beneath; 10_4 puts guesses on a line; 11_5 shares the
  corner three ways (each colour plus a third of it is exactly one third);
  14_6 cuts the sliver along the grid and slides its 17 pieces into the
  empty tile. Where the first draft asserted, the picture now shows.
- **The cold open tells the truth** (R3, D1, M13, C12). Two slabs are cut, the
  words say so, and the seams stay visible.
- **The circle's 31st region can be seen** (C2). The first draft's six points
  left a region at 0.04 percent of the disc, about 28 pixels at 4K and 3 on
  the drafts. `model::circle::DRAWN`, `oracle.py` and the fact sheet carry
  points whose smallest region is 0.97 percent, pinned by a sampled test, and
  02_4 says "spread unevenly".
- **Words that were false as maxims or overclaims are gone** (R6, R7, R9,
  D16, A1, A2, A3, A4, A17): "nobody can draw a block with four sides",
  "checking is not proof", "five checks prove nothing" (now "about the
  sixth"), "same outline", "a hundred layers on", and a note that no number of
  stacks makes a box of whole cubes (six do: 4 by 5 by 9).
- **The quiz about stacks is honest** (R4, A1). It names the rule and asks
  for the fewest; three whole stacks cannot make a box, three with the top
  step sliced can.
- **One word, one idea** (M7, R18, D14). A stack's squares are "levels",
  "layer" is the L, "tile" is the unit in chapter 14; colours follow §3
  (decision 20).
- **The model follows**: countdowns of 05_4, 12_4 and 14_4 are 8 s, 07_2 is
  7 s (`model::QUIZZES`, `oracle.py`, fact sheet), `F-K.arm.7` and `F-K.arm.8`
  for 06_3's strips, `F-L.sliver.pieces` and `F-L.sliver.fold` for the fold.
- **Length.** The fixes added words; chapters are all at most 85 s by the
  gate's estimate and the video is 17.1 min against the order's 12 to 16,
  with the next cuts named (chapter 10 into 11 and 12_7 to the description,
  16.0 min); the owner decides.
