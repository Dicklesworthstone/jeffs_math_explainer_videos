# Maya, 14: R1 storyboard review of *The Arithmetic Where 3 × 7 = 9*

I read the persona prompt and the whole R1 packet (brief, storyboard, fact sheet). I played each chapter in my head beat by beat and read the numbers aloud. I edited no files.

## What I checked and how

- **Worked by hand, then confirmed with a quick calculator run:**
  - 1% of a million = 10,000.
  - The cards: 5⊕7 = 2, 2⊕7 = 5, and A⊕C = 2, B⊕C⊕D = 6, A⊕B = 6, A⊕B⊕C⊕D = 3, B⊕C = 4, D = 6⊕4 = 2.
  - 7/8 · 6/8 · 4/8 = 168/512 = 0.328.
  - 2²⁰ ≈ a million.
  - Ring cards 6, 4, 5, 14, 9, which ex-or to 0.
  - On the 12-hour clock 9 + 5 → 2. With wrap-around, 2·131 = 262 → 6. On the 7-hour clock 3·5 → 1.
  - In the new arithmetic: (x+1)(x²+x+1) = x³+1 = 9; 2·128 = 29; 2·142 = 1; 128·4 = 58 (by doubling twice and by 7 + 2 clicks); 3·5 = 15.
  - The doubling list 1, 2, 4, … 128, 29, 58, 116, 232, 205, and 255 steps before it comes back to 1.
  - log 3 = 25 and log 150 = 180, so 150·3 = 167. 87·131 = 49 (193 under the other polynomial).
  - 0.39%/256 ≈ 0.0015%. 50,000³/3 ≈ 4.2×10¹³.
  - c = 15⊕14⊕6 = 7, so A, B, D, E = 5, 3, 2, 12. E⊕2A = 6, and all five ring cards ex-or to 15 = 3A, so A = 5.
  - 6,316/256 ≈ 24.7.
- **Checked the cube picture:** of the 7 possible two-card lit sets in 03, 6 lie flat and 1 does not (see M22).
- **Unchecked (taken from the fact sheet):** 97,876; 2.64 loops; the 29% / 71% limit for many pieces; 42 / 23 / 12%; 0.646%; 70.7%; 98–99% stall; 6,316 / 24 / 1; 255 hours being a full cycle under the other polynomial (51); "ten to sixteen".
