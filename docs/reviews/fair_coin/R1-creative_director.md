# R1 review (storyboard): fair_coin, creative director

Reviewed: `docs/reviews/fair_coin/R1-packet.md` (brief, storyboard, fact sheet), against plan §5.1, §6.1, §6.2, §6.4 and §7.5.

No blockers. Eleven majors, ten minors, four delights. Weakest chapter: **04_the_price**. Strongest: **02_toss_twice**.

## What I checked and how

- **Read in full:** my reviewer prompt, the packet, and the plan sections above.
- **Counted by script:** 1,420 spoken words, and chapter lengths that sum to 650 s (10 min 50 s). Time marks below come from the stated chapter lengths; beat lengths use the estimator's 2.68 words a second (`tools/storyboard.py`).
- **Computed by script:** pairwise CIEDE2000 for the role colours (my own implementation, sRGB under D65, which reproduces one published test pair at 2.0425). The repository's test may differ by a few tenths.
- **Worked by hand:**
  - which side of the diagonal each player's block sits on, in the coin's square and the die's;
  - the flipped-block overlaps at 05_2 and 05_4;
  - the brick 0.1 × 0.2 × 0.5 = 1%, ties at 31.5%, and the six-six block at 25%;
  - row four's shares, and 2 ÷ coloured share = 4, 6.25 and 101;
  - the first eight games parsed from `E-S2.trick.first_games`.
- **Unchecked:** every other number, every seeded count, every citation and quote. Nothing is rendered or scored yet, so no frame or sound was judged. I did not check engine cost for the pictures I propose (a second 3D segment, panning in the score engine).

## Key picture per chapter

- **01_call_it:** The coin with both faces masked by question marks while the 791 and 209 bars trade sides (01_4); the cold open itself (01_1) has no image of the promise it makes.
- **02_toss_twice:** The unit square cut at four fifths both ways, the two thin blocks reading 16% each and then taking teal and violet (02_6, 02_7).
- **03_every_coin:** The teal block lifting, flipping across the gold diagonal and landing on the violet one (03_2), then staying its mirror image as the slider drags both cuts (03_4).
- **04_the_price:** None that the chapter owns; the nearest is the histogram's tail with the thirty-pair game drawn in full (04_5), competing with four other kinds of picture (C7).
- **05_where_it_breaks:** The staggered square, whose two columns are cut at different heights (05_4): the step is the memory.
- **06_same_counts:** Patterns of two, three and four tosses sliding into groups and leaving only their sizes (06_4, 06_5); the right picture, but built in about five seconds and never touching the square.
- **07_a_die_from_a_coin:** Six four-toss patterns morphing into the six faces of a die (07_2).
- **08_loaded_dice:** The square cut six ways, one triangle of blocks folding across the gold diagonal onto the other, then collapsing back into the coin's square (08_3, 08_4).
- **09_six_bricks:** Six bricks sliding out of the cube and turning until they stand side by side, identical (09_3).
- **10_what_is_left:** A green line at 72 with the trick's 16 far beneath it (10_2).
- **11_rigorously_unbiased:** The 80% square returning with its gold mirror line (11_5), with von Neumann's paragraph (11_1) second.

## Verdict on the questions asked

- **Cold open:** a promise, not a puzzle; the picture shows only the problem (C1).
- **Payoff preview:** yes, on honest data at 02_8 and 02_9 (about 2:05 to 2:25), but not in the first 20 seconds.
- **Continuity of the square:** on stage in 02, 03, 04, 05, 08 and 11; absent from 5:45 to 7:50, exactly where the generalization is built (C3).
- **Arc:**
  - The first peak is 02_6 to 03_2, and it has no breath (C2).
  - The sag is 3:30 to 5:45 (C7, C8).
  - The second peak lands today at 07_2, not at 08_4 or 09_3 where it is meant to (C4, C5).
  - The coda dips again at 11_2 and 11_3 (C9).
- **Chapters 06 to 09:** the logic is inevitable; the pictures are not. Act III uses eight visual vocabularies in three and a half minutes, and the two named pictures never meet (C3, C4, C5).
- **Decoration:** 01_5's three pictures, 03_4's result tiles, 04_4's curve, 05_5's citation card, and 09_1's grid of 216 boxes.
- **Spectacle:** the sliders carry an idea every time (03_4, 05_2, 06_6, 04_6). The streams of games carry one at 02_8, 05_2 and 07_3 and are wallpaper by chapter 08 (C13). The bricks carry one only if they answer the question instead of following it (C4).
- **Colour:** the roles are sound; C6, C10, C12 and C15 are the problems.
- **Ending, title, sound:** C9, C20, C11.
- **Length:** the cuts below return about 30 s and the additions spend about 10.
