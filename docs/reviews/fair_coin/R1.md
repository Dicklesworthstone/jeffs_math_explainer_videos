# R1 dispositions — fair_coin (Video E)

Round R1 on the storyboard (plan §5.2), 2026-10-12, against the storyboard at
commit `2c90b12`. Reviews (verbatim, file paths made repository-relative):
`R1-maya_14.md` (M), `R1-dev_28.md` (D), `R1-ruth_45.md` (R),
`R1-engine_feasibility.md` (E), `R1-creative_director.md` (C).

Dispositions: **fixed** (in `explainer_corpus/fair_coin/docs/storyboard.md`;
beat ids are the revised ones), **answered** (why not, one sentence), or
**deferred** (to a bead: `.5` is the script and `docs/description.md`, `.6`
the build, `.8` narration and score). Gate G2r: no finding without a
disposition; no open blocker.

**Blockers:** one, raised twice (D1, R1): 10_3's words described only half of
Peres's procedure; coded as spoken it levels off near 18 fair tosses per
hundred, while the bars beside it climb to 72. Fixed at 10_4, which names
both things the procedure reuses, with a picture of each. No other blocker.

**What changed overall.** Five reviewers found, independently, that Alice's
colour sat above the diagonal in the coin's square and below it in the dice
grid (M1, D2, R14, E8, C6): the die's stretches now run from six down to one,
so a bigger roll is drawn nearer the corner, as heads is, and green stays
above the line everywhere. The square now stays on stage through the dice act
(C3): three tosses give it a third side, and the same cube returns for three
rolls. Three rolls have a chapter of their own (C4, M3). The coda opens with
von Neumann's paragraph and its price, so the price is not reopened at the
end (C9). Chapter 04 is carried by the square (C7) and chapter 05's memory is
one picture and two beats (C8). The orchestrator's one fix (06_2 said "order
never changes a chance" a minute after 05 showed a coin where it does) is
R4 and M12: the claim is fenced at 06_3 and in the edge test's name
(`edge_same_counts_same_chance_without_memory`, which now also shows the
claim failing for the coin with memory).

The storyboard went from 10.5 to 11.4 min by the G2 estimate, still 11
chapters, each ≤ 85 s (the longest is 02 at 83.8 s). Facts: 272 keys,
`facts-check` PASS.

## After R1: what the claim walk changed (P3)

The script's claim walk (`explainer_corpus/fair_coin/docs/claims.md`) moved
some lines again; where a row above quotes words, the storyboard is the
record of what is said now. In particular: the hook (M10, R7) is "a call is
fair only if the caller has no idea which side the coin favours, and neither
of them can check what the other knows", with no "Alice knows, Bob
suspects"; the fixed-length-rule argument (M19, D19, C7, R8) left the voice
for the 04_6 lower-third and the description, because its spoken form was
false for a fair coin and the chapter needed the time; 10_4 (D1, R1, R2) is
one beat that says "again and again on the two things it ignored" and "they
stop just short of it", with the two things named on the picture; M8's
sentence about a fair coin's wobble is the band's caption; D9's "one bit for
every 6 raw samples" is in the description only; and chapter 06's prediction
has a beat of its own, so its rows are 06_1 to 06_7 with the claim at 06_4
and the definition of "counts" at 06_5.
