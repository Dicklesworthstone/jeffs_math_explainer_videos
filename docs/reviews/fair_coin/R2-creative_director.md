# R2 review (scratch-voice drafts): fair_coin, creative director

No blockers. Ten majors, eleven minors, four delights. The square holds its place and colours well through 02 to 05, 08 and 11. It loses its identity at the two moments it is meant to grow (06_2, 09_1), and the video ends on a text card. Times are chapter-local seconds at scratch timing; sizes are at 960×540.

## Key picture per chapter

- **01_call_it:** The lopsided blue bar with a level green-and-violet bar growing under it, captioned "the same coin" (01_1); it reads.
- **02_toss_twice:** It should be the two thin blocks reading 4 and 4, but what stays is the square full of dots beside the two bars (02_9), because the blocks cannot be seen as blocks when they are counted (C3).
- **03_every_coin:** The green copy folding across the gold line onto the violet block, with 4/5 and 1/5 riding on it (03_2).
- **04_the_price:** The square at 99%, all grey, the colour two hairlines, reading 101 (04_5).
- **05_where_it_breaks:** The square with a step in its cut and the gold equals struck through in red (05_3); the red overhang of 05_2 is a close second.
- **06_same_counts:** Three slabs out of the cube in three orientations, each over its product in blue and pink, then turned until identical (06_4, t≈32–36).
- **07_a_die_from_a_coin:** Six four-toss patterns, each over its die face, with the other boxes greyed (07_2).
- **08_loaded_dice:** The square cut six ways, green above and violet below the gold line, folding as one sheet and then closing into the coin's square (08_4, 08_5).
- **09_six_orders:** Six bricks tumbling out of the glass cube mid-turn (09_3, t≈24.5); that frame lasts about a second (queued item 1).
- **10_what_is_left:** The ruled line at 72.2 with the trick's 16 far below it (10_3).
- **11_the_fair_coin_inside:** The 80% square with its gold line opening into the six-way grid and closing (11_3), though the last five seconds of the video are a text card (C6).
