# R1 engine-feasibility review: fair_coin storyboard

**Reviewer:** engine_feasibility. **Pin:** franken_manim `a399d626`, read in cargo's checkout `~/.cargo/git/checkouts/franken_manim-6641d0e09642f63e/a399d62`. I built, rendered and edited nothing. Any fps or pixel figure not credited to a spike is my own estimate, and says so.

**Verdict:** there are no blockers. Every picture can be built at the pin. Eleven majors would change how a beat is built, and two of them change what the viewer sees: the 04_5 tail and the 07_3 stream.

**Two corrections to my brief:**
- `three_customers.rs` is **not** on the camera route. Its module doc says the cube "is drawn on the retained route, orthographically… (The camera route samples every stroke pixel in 3-D, far too slow for 200 trails.)" It projects lattice points itself (`Pose::project`, `Cube::install`). The only chapters that really use the camera route are raptorq `chapters/cube.rs` and capture_recapture `chapters/landscape.rs`; I found them with a grep for `request_camera` and `kit.camera()`.
- The reviewer prompt lists S-2, S-3, S-4, S-6 and S-8 as open. Plan §6.6 records all of them as done on 2026-10-11, so no beat here waits on a spike. The one probe this storyboard needs is for chapter 09 (E1).

## Key pictures, chapter by chapter

- **01:** A lopsided coin drops discs into a row, then 1,000 tosses race into two bars that stop at 791 and 209. Feasible: individual discs for the first ~20, then a tick grid feeding bars resized by placement (E16).
- **02:** The unit square cut at 0.8 on both axes, readouts 64/16/16/4, then 1,000 games. Feasible with one square prop moved by placement (E4). The "rows race" has to become a 1,000-cell strip (E16).
- **03:** The two thin blocks stay mirror images while the slider sweeps from 50% to 99%; one block flips across the gold diagonal; numbers morph into letters. Feasible. As specified, `TransformMatchingTex` pairs single glyphs rather than whole factors (E5); the flip should use a ghost copy (E13).
- **04:** Blocks merge into a proportion bar, then a 10,000-game histogram, a U-curve with a broken axis, and the long tail. Feasible, except that the tail is below one pixel (E7).
- **05:** The square with its own top cut, then the staggered square. Feasible with the same prop if it has three trackers (E4).
- **06:** Disc patterns sort into Pascal's groups, and row four becomes a bar the slider re-cuts. Feasible, but the 0- and 1-head slices are a pixel or less in 540p drafts (E9).
- **07:** Six patterns morph into die faces, and 24,000 tosses make 949 rolls. Feasible as a lit grid plus bars, not as a literal stream (E6).
- **08:** The 36-block grid with its teal and violet triangles, a block flip, a triangle flip, and a morph between the grid and the coin's square. Feasible, but the colour sides contradict the coin square (E8) and one block is mislabelled (E17).
- **09:** A sliced cube with six congruent bricks that slide out and line up under an orbit. Feasible on the camera route at about 6–7 fps at 4K (my estimate, roughly +4 min per final), with labels only while the camera is still (E1–E3, E12). It is cheaper and more legible done the way bakery 06 actually drew its cube.
- **10:** A 100-disc strip, a 0–100 scale with the green 72, and two bar ladders. Feasible from primitives (E22).
- **11:** Verbatim quote cards, the square at 50%, an Intel pair-filter stream, the square again, and the receipts card. Feasible, but bakery's `quote_card` would silently cut the quote, and the source line overflows the frame (E11).

## Cost of the heavy beats (my estimates)

- **Square chapters (02–05, 08, 11):** about 25 mobjects. While a tracker moves, each frame costs at most 10 placement ops and 5 readout rebuilds; still frames are cached. S-8 measured 18 live numbers plus 2 maps at 36–46 CPU-ms per frame, with no growth over 80 s, so the ≤ 8 live readouts here are safe.
- **Grid and strip beats** (01_3: 25 strips; 02_8: 20; 07_3: 60 × 100 cells): each frame recolours at most 2 rows. That is far under capture's 100-strip grid, which already rendered.
- **06_5:** 24 pattern strips (88 discs), at most 24 moves per play. **08_3–08_4:** 36 blocks, 15 ghosts and 36 morph targets.
- **09 on the camera route:** 6 `Prism`s (each a group of 6 faces, `solids.rs`), ≤ 72 lines, 12 edges and ~8 HUD texts. That compares with S-5's 484 prisms at 6.1 fps at 4K60, and stills at 7.2 fps; the floor is full-frame CPU rendering with no tile cache. Expect 6–7 fps: ~2,400 frames ≈ 6–7 min, about +4 min over the retained route. The `three_customers` method runs at the retained route's speed.
- **Whole video:** ~37,800 frames at ~15 fps (the x264 limit on this Mac) ≈ 42 min, plus ≈ 4 min for 09: about 46–50 min per 4K60 final. That is well within budget; 540p drafts are a fraction of it. Every chapter is ≤ 85 s.

## What I verified in code, and what I am inferring

**Verified in code (kit and corpus):**
- `kit.rs`: `text` sets no font family; `live_*` and `live_bar` use `always_redraw`; `text_hud`; `camera_to`.
- `props.rs`: `lower_third` geometry and its 92 × 3 wrap; `receipts_card` does not wrap; `histogram`, `grow_histogram`, `RowStrips`, `glow`.
- `animatic.rs::wrap` truncates silently; `layout.rs::Linter::check` checks only frame edges and text overlaps; `plot.rs::rect` clamps sizes; `encode.rs::render_camera`.
- Corpus: `three_customers.rs` (retained route, `Cube::install`); raptorq `cube.rs`; capture `landscape.rs`; `flag.rs::ribbons`; bakery `draw.rs::quote_card` and `Sign::flip`; capture `cheating.rs::lighting` and `pile_up`; capture `ring_toss.rs`, whose slider is stepped (`.animate().move_to` plus `replacement_transform`), not tracker-driven.

**Verified in the engine at the pin:**
- `positional.rs`: `apply_affine_many` dedupes with `contains`, so group ops are O(M²); `stretch_about` composes a world-space affine onto the placement; `rotate`.
- `animate.rs`: flip, rotate-about and apply-matrix are recorded as Transform targets.
- `text_matching.rs::tex_pairs`; `tex.rs` makes one child per glyph.
- `rotation.rs::Rotating` restores from its starting copy every frame; `uniforms.rs` sets `depth_test: false` by default.
- `retained.rs` refuses non-vector programs; `solids.rs` builds `Prism` as a group of faces; `camera.rs` has field-of-view and focal-distance setters.
- `numbers.rs` has `DecimalNumber::unit`; `transform.rs` has `align_data_and_family` and `with_path_arc`.

**Inferred, not verified:**
- That digits in a number get one span key each.
- How the retained route draws points with z ≠ 0.
- How `Transform` handles a stretched placement.
- The cost of 3D strokes, and the fps for chapter 09.
- Whether lighting follows a rotated placement.
- Whether a dot-batching API exists.
- Whether `\%`, "š" and italic "…" render.
