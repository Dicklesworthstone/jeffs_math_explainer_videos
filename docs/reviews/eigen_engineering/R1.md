# R1 dispositions — eigen_engineering (Video K)

Round R1 on the storyboard (plan §5.2), 2026-10-13, against the storyboard at
commit `fab5fbd`. Reviews (verbatim): `R1-maya_14.md` (M), `R1-dev_28.md` (D),
`R1-ruth_45.md` (R), `R1-engine_feasibility.md` (E), `R1-creative_director.md`
(C). 153 findings.

Dispositions: **fixed** (in `explainer_corpus/eigen_engineering/docs/storyboard.md`,
`src/`, `oracle.py` or the fact sheet; beat ids are the **revised** ones),
**answered** (why not, one sentence), or **deferred** (to a bead: `.5` the
script and description, `.6` the build, `.8` narration and score). Gate G2r:
no finding without a disposition; no open blocker. The revision follows the
owner's workflow of 2026-10-13: `docs/protected.tsv` lists what every later
revision keeps, and `docs/decisions.md` records each choice made here.

**Blockers, all fixed.**
- **R1** (the ending contradicted Dallard et al.: the design model had the
  sideways modes within 10%; the load that fed them was what nobody
  expected). 14_6 and 14_7 now say exactly that and end on three questions;
  the brief's hook row is rewritten to match; the 10% and the damper counts
  are facts (`K-B2.predicted_within_percent`, `.tmd_lateral_pairs`).
- **C1** (the hero picture vanished for nine minutes). Two devices now recur
  in every act: the arrow test (03, the truss at 06_1 and 06_2, the air at
  09_3, the economy at 12_5 and 13_2 to 13_4, the quarter turn at 14_5) and
  the ladder of own patterns (04_3, 06_2, 06_5, 13_4, 14_1). New facts give
  their readouts (`K-P3.lone_turn_deg` 64°, `K-P3.softest_turn_deg` 0°,
  `K-R1.lump_turn_deg` 35.3°).
- **E1** (superscript letters in picture cells have no glyph and would stop
  the animatics). Every formula in the picture and features columns is now
  TeX source or plain text; none of ᵀ ᵏ ᵢ ⱼ ⁻ remains.

**What changed overall.** The factory chapter is folded into one picture in
the coda's list of limits (C5, M18; the video is 14 chapters); its countdown
and three others became plain pauses or went, and a quiz was added where the
split happens (C13): seven countdowns and seven plain pauses. The cold open
puts the swaying deck in frame one and reaches the title at about 20 s (C4).
The economy act now uses the split on a lopsided order whose rounds swing
onto the even mix (C9, R8, D8, M15). "Mode" waits until mass is counted
(M10, R6, D4). The rooms of chapter 11 have no sides in simple ratios, so
only the cube stacks its tones (R9, D7), and the cell method now runs on a
small attic studio whose modes are audible (R17, M17, C10). Length went from
16.7 to 17.6 min by the G2 estimate; the cut list is in `decisions.md` D-10.
