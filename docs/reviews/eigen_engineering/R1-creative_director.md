## What I checked, and how

- **Read in full:** the persona prompt; plan §5.1, §6.1 to §6.4, §7.2 and §7.3; the whole packet (brief, 15 chapters and 93 beats with notes, fact sheet with findings, terms, sources and every fact table); `explainer_corpus/eigen_engineering/src/score.rs`. - **Timing:** I reproduced the gate's rule in a scratch script outside the repository. It gives 1,000.9 s = 16.68 min, 93 beats, 2,179 spoken words. Every timestamp and duration below comes from that; they are estimates, not measurements. The ten rings total 51 s.
- **Recomputed by hand:**
  - The truss's tone ratios from `K-V1.hz_2dp`: 1 : 1.993 : 2.514.
  - 4.3 against 5.91 Hz is about 550 cents down.
  - 71.5 against 68.6 Hz is 72 cents apart and beats 2.9 times a second.
  - A box mode's floor pattern does not depend on the count along the height.
  - The split run forwards on the move (1, 0) returns (2, −1).
  - The economy's rounds from a demand of (30, 10) are (15, 11), (9.3, 8.5), (6.27, 6.11), totalling (75, 50), which equals `K-E3.other_total`.
  - At 60 fps, 14.9 Hz is four frames a swing.
  - At 84 BPM, five seconds is exactly seven beats; four and six seconds are not whole beats.
- **Counted from the picture column:** the beats that show the arrow plane; 17 lower-thirds (5 are FrankenSim file paths); 22 card beats of 93; about two dozen numbers on screen in chapter 05.
- **Unchecked:** every other number in the storyboard and fact sheet; the sources; whether the engine can afford what I propose (camera route at 4K, 2,246 dots, 21 trusses redrawn, motes). The Schroeder and Kuttruff statistics in C7 are from memory. Nothing is rendered and no cue sheet exists, so "cue against voice" is judged from intent only.

## Verdict

- **Hero picture:** there is one and it is the right one, but it is a first-act picture. The arrow plane is on screen for about 100 s of 1,001 and is absent from 4:01 to 13:13. Each act has its own central picture, and the split is drawn four different ways (C1, C2, C11, C12).
- **Cold open:** the first 20 s are a plan view, a ruler and a stamp. The paradox lands at 0:25, the payoff preview at 0:46 and the title at 0:55.5, against the brief's "at most 20 s" (C4).
- **Ending:** the last line lands; the last picture is a still deck with a damper count. Chapter 15 is eight beats and eight pictures (C3).
- **Talking slideshow:** 13 is the worst; 12 until 12_6; the second half of 09 (36 of 76 s are cards and a sum); 10 and 11 are chart chapters; 15 is a list. From 08_7 (8:48) to 11_5 (12:00) nothing moves except charts, and from 12:15 to the end only two sweeps do (C9, C10, C17, C22).
- **Length:** 18.2 min at the 9% Video E's drafts ran over. I would cut chapter 14 first (C5).
- **Quizzes:** five of the ten rings are delights. 02_6 and 03_1 are one sum asked twice, 17 s apart across a chapter wipe. The last three fall inside 2:14. The split itself has no ring (C13).
- **Colour:** blue, orange and gold carry meaning throughout. Magenta and teal appear in two beats of 93, and the mode maps name no colour (C16, C18).
- **Sound:** no teaching listen window is committed, in a video with an act on acoustics. I propose three (C6, C7); cues to keep off the voice are in C20.
- **Spectacle:** the showcase is seven everyday 2D features. One camera shot is worth it, the room of 10_1 and 10_2; an orbit round the truss is not (C8, C10, C14).

## Key picture, chapter by chapter

- **01_the_bridge_that_swayed:** the deck seen from above, swinging 70 mm under walking dots (01_2). It sits behind a date, a crowd caption, a ruler and a stamp, and nothing in it is gold yet (C4).
- **02_two_carts:** both carts sliding together while the middle spring does not change, and the two lists pulsing gold (02_7).
- **03_own_patterns:** the blue arrow swept round the circle with the orange one running ahead and falling behind, lying along it on two gold lines (03_3 to 03_5). This is the hero picture; its key frame as specified hides one arrow under the other (C11).
- **04_the_split:** the orange arrow (1, 0) splitting along the two gold lines, each piece divided on its own, the blue pieces adding tip to tail to (2/3, 1/3) (04_2 to 04_4).
- **05_a_steel_truss:** the members taking colour, magenta and thick along the top, teal along the bottom, the middle post thin and grey (05_7). The table filling a member at a time (05_3) loses to its own clutter (C21).
- **06_twenty_one_patterns:** the load breaking into 21 bars and the top of the ladder all but vanishing when each is divided by its stiffness (06_4). The chapter's title image, the 21 patterns, is three small trusses played in turn (C14).
- **07_every_mode_a_note:** three copies of the truss swinging side by side at three rates (07_3). It is silent, and at true speed it strobes (C6, C15).
- **08_two_bridges:** the walker counter climbing to 156 over a still deck, then ten more and the sway taking off (08_6).
- **09_the_air_in_a_room:** the slices of air turning into the row of carts and springs (09_2).
- **10_two_million_modes:** peaks filling the frequency line, sparse then dense, until they merge at 20 Hz (10_6). The box of 10_1 cannot show the count along the height (C8).
- **11_where_modes_rule:** the hall's side view cut into 306 cells with a mode on it (11_5). It is the only picture in the act that looks like a concert hall, and it gets one line at the end of the chapter (C10).
- **12_an_economy:** the arrow plane again, food across and tools up, a gold line along the even mix (12_6).
- **13_can_it_feed_itself:** stacks of blocks, each seven tenths of the one under it, closing on the dashed line at 80 (13_3). It is a bar chart; no picture here is the video's own (C9).
- **14_a_different_tool:** the line of equal profit sliding outward and last touching the region at (40, 20) (14_3).
- **15_one_idea:** none. The best candidate, the quarter turn whose orange arrow stays at right angles all the way round, gets half a frame for about 3 s. This is a major finding; C3 proposes the image.
