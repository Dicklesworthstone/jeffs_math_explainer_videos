# Creative-director review, R1 storyboards: Videos A, B and C

**What I read:** the reviewer prompt; all three R1 packets in full (brief, storyboard and fact sheet each); plan §6 and §7, plus the engineering notes in §9.7, §10.7 and §11.7. I also read the round-1 creative memo from the plan review, to see what revision 2 adopted. It adopted most of it: the table hero, the danger meter, the byte clock, the lanes and progress square, the 08-on-07 overlay, and listen windows.

**What I recomputed** (scratch scripts only; nothing in the repo was touched):
- **Line timings**, at the storyboard's own rate. My totals reproduce 12.3, 13.3 and 11.8 min.
- **Cold-open lengths.**
- **Video B's byte-chord voicing:** the spacing between adjacent notes (12-TET, A4 = 440 Hz), and the notes per second of its sixteenth-note arpeggios.
- **The GF(2)³ cube:** which of the seven two-card spans are real planes, by determinants.
- **The byte clock:** that every byte's partner (inverse) under 0x11D is its mirror image on the 255-hour clock. All 255 bytes checked.
- **Video A's proposed mosaic:** that its dividers line up exactly when Mn = kN. By algebra, and at Feller's numbers.
- **Colour distances** (CIEDE2000) for Video C's colour pairs. The storyboard gives no hex values for the customers, so I assumed the 3b1b ones.

**What I did not check:** I did not recompute any fact-sheet number; they are unchecked, not "fine". No frames exist yet, so every visual judgement is about the storyboard text.

**Rubric:** not applicable; it is for the persona reviewers only.

**The five changes that matter most:**
1. **A:** draw the hero table with cell areas in proportion to the counts (a mosaic). "Balance" then becomes a visible straight line. This one picture removes 06's seesaws and 09's montage (A-1).
2. **B:** the byte chord has no listen window anywhere, and its storyboarded voicing fails the plan's own roughness check (B-1 to B-3). The doubling of 128 can be heard twice: as silence in 05 (the bit falls off), and as the 0x1D chord in 06 (the bit is folded back in). That is the best teaching sound in the whole set.
3. **C:** promote the three-customer cube into a storyboard row. Also make the viewer hear "taking turns" before the clash at 05_3 (C-1, C-2).
4. **All four videos use the same chord progression** (I–vi–IV–V), only in different keys. Give each its own harmonic language, and map the harmony act by act (X-1).
5. **B, chapter 07:** thin it out. Give the 256×256 table reorder a stretch with no narration, then a hold (B-4).
