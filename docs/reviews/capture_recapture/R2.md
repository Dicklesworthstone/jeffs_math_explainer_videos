  share of marked fish as the lake. Then it's a fair copy." → "Every failure
  breaks one rule. On average, the second catch must hold the same share of
  marked fish as the lake on marking day."

  still holds." → "The estimate doesn't move. Death doesn't care about marks, so
  the share stays one in ten. But it counts the lake on marking day, not the
  seven thousand left."

  is biased exactly when that share differs from the marked share of the lake
  you mean" → "precisely: Mn/k = M ÷ (marked share of the second catch). It
  aims at a lake of L fish (Mn/E[k] = L) exactly when that share is, on
  average, M/L. Even then it runs a little high (chapter 09)."
- Keys `A-CH13.*`: targets Mn/E[k] 10,000 (ideal), 10,000 (deaths), 12,000
