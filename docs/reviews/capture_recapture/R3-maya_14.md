# R3 review: Maya, 14, on the capture_recapture narrated draft

**Verdict:** I'd watch this to the end, and most of it I could explain to a friend. No chapter left me with an empty sentence, so there are no blockers. The one place I'd probably quit is chapter 7, when the letters take over. Chapter 6 is the other weak spot: I understood the words but couldn't see the picture they were describing. Three findings are majors (06_3, 07_3–07_5, 14_5). The rest are small wording or layout fixes. Chapters 2, 3, 4, 11 and 12 are the best maths video I've watched this year.

## What I checked and how

- I read my persona prompt and the whole packet: stage instructions, the script and the description.
- I opened all 15 cue sheets and their legends. Each sheet tiles the frame 1.5 s into every line, in script order, and pads the last row with copies of its last frame. I looked at every tile a second time cropped and enlarged 2×, so I could judge small text the way I'd see it on a phone. I didn't complain about the draft resolution itself.
- A single frame can't show what appears later in a line. For those cases I read the chapter sources to see what comes in (marked "per source" below). The sources may be a few commits behind the renders.
- The packet also lists three dense contact sheets for chapter 15. They weren't in the sheets folder I was given, so I didn't review them.
- Arithmetic I redid in my head and that came out right: 1,000×1,000÷100 = 10,000. 10×10÷3 ≈ 33. 10+10−3 = 17. 20·19·18·17 = 116,280, and ÷24 = 4,845. The piles 1,365 + 2,275 + 1,050 + 150 + 5 = 4,845, and 10 × 105 = 1,050. 1,365/4,845 ≈ 28%, 2,275/4,845 ≈ 47%. 23×23 = 529, 33×16 = 528, 24×24 = 576, 34×17 = 578. 100 − 99 = 1 = 49 − 48. 100 × 8,100 = 810,000 = 900 × 900. 1,000,000÷90 ≈ 11,100 and ÷110 ≈ 9,100. 51×51÷1−1 = 2,600. 50×50÷1,000 = 2.5. 1,000×1,000÷1,000,000 = 1. 30×24÷12 = 60, 30+24−12 = 42, 60−42 = 18.
- Every other number is unchecked. Ruth checks the maths. My "correctness" score only means whether something *sounded* wrong to me.
