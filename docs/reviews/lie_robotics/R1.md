# R1 dispositions — lie_robotics (Video G)

Round R1 on the storyboard (plan §5.2), 2026-10-13, against the storyboard at
commit `5e76d40`. Reviews (verbatim, except that each reviewer's scratch
folder is written as `<scratch>`): `R1-maya_14.md` (M), `R1-dev_28.md` (D),
`R1-ruth_45.md` (R), `R1-engine_feasibility.md` (E),
`R1-creative_director.md` (C). 160 findings: 0 blockers, 44 major, 90 minor,
26 delights.

Dispositions: **fixed** (in `explainer_corpus/lie_robotics/docs/storyboard.md`
unless another file is named; beat ids are the revised ones), **answered**
(why not, one sentence), or **deferred** (to a bead: `.5` is the script and
the description, `.6` the build, `.8` narration and score). Gate G2r: no
finding without a disposition; no open blocker.

**What the reviewers checked.** Maya, Dev and Ruth each recomputed the
numbers independently (their own NumPy and SciPy scripts, their own arm
models): every value they reached matched the fact sheet at the shown
precision, including Newton's table to its last digit. What they found
wrong was in the words and the pictures.

**What changed overall.**

- **The lost direction now matches its count** (M9, D4, R1, C6). The quiz
  of chapter 11 showed the arm stretched straight out, where the readout
  falls from 6 to 4 while the words explained one direction, twice. The
  pose is now the one with only the elbow straight: rank 5, one lost
  direction, a slide along the arm (`G-J.rank.elbow_straight`,
  `G-J.elbow_straight.push_along_arm` = 0, with 0.29 at a bent pose so the
  test can fail). The note that the stretched arm is "inside its reach" was
  false and is gone; a singular pose that is inside the reach is named on
  screen (`G-J.rank.elbow_only`, 0.58 m of 0.82). The residual that mixed
  radians and metres (R16) is replaced by that push, in m/s per rad/s.
- **The wrist's callback no longer teaches something false** (M10, D12,
  R7). 11_6 adds "A six-joint arm would lose a direction here. The seventh
  joint covers for it", with "directions: 6" lit. Ruth's counterexample is
  now a fact (`G-J.rank.ell_wrist_turned` = 5) and the fact sheet's finding
  9 no longer says "never".
- **The gimbal is drawn in the model's order** (R6, D7, E14). The picture
  had its middle and inner rings exchanged; for the drawn order the two
  orientations of 13_2 would be 180° apart, not 1.4°. `G-E.rings.order`.
- **One move, kept on screen** (M2, M3, M11, C1, C3, C4, R3). Chapter 04
  uses one ruler: wrapped on the circle it is the trap, peeled off and laid
  flat with its zero on the first heading it is the cure. That circle and
  line become a corner badge that lights whenever the move is made (05_3,
  06_4, 10_2, 13_3, 14_3, 14_4, 15_4). The platform's 3 m straight line is
  no longer a crossed-out wrong answer: it is the flat piece's answer, and
  it bends at constant length into the arc. Chapter 07's screw grows out of
  chapter 06's floor (the gold centre stands up into the axis). Chapter 13
  runs the badge once per Newton step and shows the error as a shrinking
  gold screw. 05_2 says outright that adding two spins is both at once, not
  one turn then another (`G-B.both_at_once`: 127°, neither order's answer).
- **Twists made readable** (D1, D2, D5, D6, R5, R14, R18, M13). A tag at
  10_3 says what a twist's slide rows are (the velocity of the point at the
  room's origin) and how to get the tip's velocity; 13_4's code panel is
  written out with its frame named; 08_1 shows the model as data and 08_2
  the rule for turning about a line; ω is a unit axis and t the angle in
  radians wherever the bracket appears; one card fixes turn, spin, screw and
  twist.
- **The bracket is called what it is** (R2, R10, M12, M15, C9). The
  leftover of two turns is a turn (the group's commutator); the Lie bracket
  is the rule on the flat piece that it follows for small turns. Chapter 15
  shows the leftover between the two books in one sweep from 90° to 1°, with
  its own axis, and says "ten times smaller, a hundredfold".
- **"Three numbers have a bad spot" is stated exactly and tested** (R4, R9,
  M4). 05_2 no longer says arrows have "no bad spots"; the lower third of
  03_6 is the theorem; the row that could not fail (`G-X6.left_below_one`)
  is replaced by all twelve orders of three rings (`G-X6.ring_orders`
  12/12).
- **Structure** (C2, C5, C12, C14, M7, M32). The title lands straight after
  the cold open's answer. The robot's real question is asked at 10_1 and the
  pink goal stays dim through chapters 10 to 12. Chapter 07 opens on its
  quiz as a prediction and ends on Chasles; the storage boxes moved to 03_6.
  Chapter 09 ends on what the recipe never asks. Chapter 13 tries
  subtracting angles first and ends on "three".
- **Palette** (C7, C8). Five pairs of the first palette were under the
  ΔE2000 20 the brief claimed (16.0 at worst). The five data hues were
  replaced (the corpus's hero, warn, good and the quiz violet kept); the
  least pair is now 20.7 and `palette::tests` computes it. Every velocity
  arrow is orange; teal is for positions and paths only.
- **Words** (M1, M35, R17, R19). The book's first turn is "tilt" (the
  gripper keeps "tip"); the trick "wraps the answer back on" (the book
  keeps "roll"); the mobile robot is "the platform" (the arm keeps
  "base"); both turns are about lines painted on the table; chapter 14 is
  "the steady path", since which path is straight depends on what is held
  constant.
- **Length.** The fixes added words; two more passes of cutting leave 16.3
  min by the gate's estimate against the 12–16 default (the brief and the
  fact sheet say so, with what the next cut would be).
