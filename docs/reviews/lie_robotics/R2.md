# R2 dispositions — lie_robotics (Video G)

Round R2 on the final script and the storyboard (plan §5.2, §5.3), 2026-10-14, after
narration: this round is about correctness and clarity, and every accepted fix is a concrete
change. Reviews (verbatim): `R2-maya_14.md` (M), `R2-dev_28.md` (D), `R2-ruth_45.md` (R, with
`ruth_audit.md` run item by item), and `R2-modes-passes.md` (P01 to P03, the mathematician
audit's three generation passes). Their 31 atomic claims that could change the video were each
audited by a reader who had not written them (`R2-claim-audit.md`: A-01 to A-10, B-01 to B-10,
C-01 to C-11). 44 + 28 + 23 findings from the three viewers (including 8 + 5 + 4 delights, kept),
and 8 claims from the passes. The packet they read is regenerated by `tools/review.py
lie_robotics script` and is not committed (its `docs/description.md` did not exist; the packet
says so, and the description is now written).

Dispositions: **fixed** (in the named file), **answered** (why not, one sentence) or **deferred**
(to the owner, with what is asked). No blocker was raised. Gate: no finding without a
disposition.

**Method.** Six fresh readers, each with its own prompt, none reading another's output or R1:
Maya, Dev and Ruth on the packet, and three bounded operators (statements about turns and
history with boundary witnesses; what a roboticist would object to, each number built from the
arm's own link offsets; what leaves a 14-year-old believing something false). Their findings are
proposals. A finding that a computation or a source could settle was settled before it was
accepted: the 31 claims went to three auditors, who rebuilt the arm from the xacro and ran their
own code. A checked null was allowed everywhere and used (P01 returned
`NO_MATERIAL_INCREMENT`). The `modes-of-reasoning-project-analysis` discipline was applied in its
lean form: named questions, independent readers, claims audited by others, no quotas; its
JSONL ledger of the run was not built: the claims are the 31 numbered statements of
`R2-claim-audit.md`, their sources are named in the tables below, and `R2.md` is the record.

**What was verified by recomputation** (own code of the readers and auditors, none of it
`oracle.py` or `model.rs`; every number the script says that could be recomputed matched): the
book's two covers and 120° turns; the leftover at 1°, 10°, 90° (0.0175°, 1.7409°, 120°) and its
axis; the platform's half circle; the arm's heights, the by-hand tips, the levers, ranks at ten

other starts); the blend's determinant and 18.4° against 22.5°; the screw and the 7.58 cm gap;

and Ceccarelli, Comanche 055's listing, KUKA's PDF (the 75°/s was confirmed through listings,
not the sheet itself). The transcript of Collins's line (04 09 00 30 to 04 09 00 46) was read.

## What changed overall

- **The spoken script changes in one line, 11_5, by one word.** "it must turn at fifty-four
  degrees per second" is now "the elbow must turn": two readers (M-24, R-06) heard the gripper,
  which does not turn. `11_5` is the only id to re-narrate. Everything else the readers found
  lands on a picture, a lower third, a card, a tag, the description or the fact sheet.
- **Two on-screen sentences were false as read, and are fixed.** 11_3's lower third said "the
  gripper can only swing sideways": what is lost is a push of the wrist's centre along the arm;
  the tip, 0.126 m past it, can still move along the arm while turning (9.2 rad/s of turning for
  1 m/s), so it now says "the wrist" (A-01, P01, P02, P03 independently). 13_6's "each step gains
  more than the one before" is false in millimeters (411, 90, 4.1 fall) and true as a factor
  (5.4, 23, 157): "each step cuts the error by a bigger factor" (R-07, A-04).
- **One thing a developer could not run.** The 13_4 panel used `J_body` that was never defined
  on screen; ch 10's grid is the space form. With one of each frame the loop does not converge
  (0 of 60 starts); with matching forms it takes 3 steps. A note in the panel defines `J_body`
  (D-01, B-01). The alternative (use the space form) was rejected: there "0.1 mm" is the room
  origin's remaining distance, not the tip's (661 mm against 505 mm at the start).
- **Missing exact versions, now on screen or in the description.** The convention of
  "halfway" (shorter way round, A-02); the senses of the book's two turns (A-03); what a
  Jacobian column is (B-02); the limit's owner and source (B-03); that singular is said of joint
  angles (B-04); the seam trap that 13_2's "both traps" promised (A-06); that the forward Euler
  step is the naive one (B-06); Lie's date beside Brockett's (B-09).
- **Motivation through 10 to 12.** The robot's real question was said to stay in view "through
  chapters 10 to 12", but only chapter 10 drew the goal. Chapters 11 and 12 now draw it, dimmed,
  under standing words, "the goal: reached in steps, each a speed held for one second" (M-07,
  C-04: "small steps" was rejected as false of this run, whose first two steps are large).
- **`docs/description.md` is written** (all three viewers: major): chapters, the exact version
  of each simplification by beat, the numbers to test with, the loop, sources, and what was not
  verified.
- **A quiz tick no longer touches its row's words** in any chapter: the strip was 0.09 wide, so
  the tick of 05_4, 06_2 and 08_3 sat against the longest labels (one test builds all fifteen
  cards).
- **The protected phrases were not touched** (42 of 42, `./x lie_robotics protected`).

## Flagged for the owner

- **"A pose like this is called a singularity" (11_4).** In the video's own vocabulary a pose is
  the gripper's (01_5, 12_4), and singular is a property of joint angles: one gripper pose can be
  reached by a singular and a non-singular set (witness in the description). The lower third now
  says "a property of the joint angles". The spoken line was not touched; a one-word change,
  "A set of joint angles like this", is available (B-04, P03-02). Low materiality.
- **"In space" (05_1, 07_1, 07_2)** after the Apollo chapter can be heard as outer space (M-15).
  The picture shows the room each time; "in three dimensions" would cost three lines.
- **"radioed a request" (03_5)** is the grammatical form of Collins's line, a joke (B-10); the
  card could carry the capcom's "You were unreadable" and his "Disregard" (optional).
- **Countdowns** of 4 s for 03_2, 07_1 and 11_2 were found short by Maya and Dev (M-32, D-20).
  Timing is set on the narrated draft; not changed here.
- **Length** (fact sheet finding 11: 16.5 min against 12–16). If cuts are wanted, Dev would keep
  03_4, 11_5 and 14_5 and take 03_5 before 03_4 (D-23); Maya's reading agrees that 03_5 is the
  act's one human moment (M-33).
- **Quizzes' ticks.** The strip that keeps a tick clear of its row's words was widened in
  `draw.rs` (0.8 to 1.0 of the scale); the long answers of 05, 06 and 08 shrink by about 5% more.
