Five paradoxes · visual prototype 01

Three possible places for the car.

You pick door 1. Should you switch after the host opens a goat door? First, make a prediction. Then reveal all three equally likely car locations.

Host rules: The host knows where the car is, always opens an unchosen door with a goat, and always offers a switch. If both unchosen doors have goats, this illustration opens door 2.

The three car locations are equally likely. This shows the overall always-switch strategy; it does not claim a probability conditional on a particular named door being opened under every possible host tie-breaking rule.
