Who commutes with a flip?

Only four square symmetries give the same result on either side of the horizontal flip:

C(s) = {e, r², s, r²s}

This set is the centralizer of s. The other four moves do not commute with s.

When does grouping moves preserve multiplication?

Compare two ways of grouping the square's moves. Choose one representative from each class and multiply them. Remember: the right-hand move happens first.
