## Packaging decisions already made

- No “always late” bus claim. The phenomenon is an expectation under a specified arrival model, not every individual wait or timetable failure.
- No wartime numerical success claim until its original historical source is checked. Serial inference works as a mathematical mystery without borrowing an inflated legend.
- “Shares” rather than literal partial keys in Shamir: a share must not look like a visible piece of the secret. A large on-screen finite-field example earns the no-information statement.
- Crofton uses a finite-length drawn curve at a stated resolution; do not imply a resolution-independent length for arbitrary coastlines.
- Parrondo must name the particular mixture/schedule tested, with capital-state dependence visible before the winning graph. The title must be earned by the proof and must not promise real-money gains.

Testing after release: compare CTR alongside its actual impressions, first-minute retention, average viewing and likes/dislikes at comparable publication ages. Do not select whichever metric flatters the result. No A/B result exists yet.
