# Caption line-wrap proposals

These are local draft alternatives for human review, not published captions. The original SRT files are retained. The manifest binds source and proposal hashes and records that each block's timestamp and flattened text remain identical.

A hash-checked scan of the current caption handoffs covered 10 algebra chapters / 99 blocks, seven exponential chapters / 71 blocks, and seven logarithm chapters / 64 blocks. A simple triage flagged lines with at most three characters, lines over 42 characters, or blocks over 20 characters per second. It found no flags in the algebra set, three one-word lines in two exponential chapters, and two in the logarithm opening. These heuristics are prompts for review, not an accessibility standard or acceptance result.

The three proposals reflow those five one-word lines without changing words, cue boundaries, or timestamps. Some captions still end mid-sentence, including at “and”; changing block boundaries requires separate timing and speech review. Confirm readability in the actual player, check the narration and synchronization, and obtain accessibility review before adopting these files. No listening, independence, or acceptance is claimed.
