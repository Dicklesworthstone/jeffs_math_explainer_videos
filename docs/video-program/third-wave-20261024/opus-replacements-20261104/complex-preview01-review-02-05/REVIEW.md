## Evidence for the high items

**04-A stripes.** `checks/stripes.py` averages each row's luma and removes a 9-row running mean.

| Patch | Row residual rms | Strongest period |
|---|---|---|
| 04 frame 20, hue only | 3.4% | 3.11 px |
| 04 frame 140, z² | 3.2% | 3.13 px |
| 04 frame 300, bump | 3.1% | 3.13 px |
| 05 frame 92, exp | 4.5% | 3.11 px |
| 05 frame 200, tan | 3.2% | 3.11 px |
| 05 frame 367, zeta | 3.3% | 3.16 px |
| 05 frame 464, zoom | 3.1% | 3.13 px |

- **Spacing:** `CELL` is 0.035 units (`draw.rs:523`), which is 3.15 native px.
- **Likely cause:** `RowStrips` draws each row as a stroke exactly one cell tall (`crates/explainer_kit/src/props.rs`). The default 1.5 px anti-alias band lets the background through at each boundary. GF256's `crisp()` comment says the same: "the default 1.5 px left a seam between strips" (`raptorq_gf256/src/chapters/times_table.rs:100–106`).
- **The complex crate already has the fix in one place:** its `thumbnail.rs:72` sets 0.5, and `Painter::build` and `portrait` do not.
- **Natural experiment in the approved film:**

| GF256 frame | Crisp? | Row size | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook picture, 4K, 4 s | No | About 3.5 px | 2.8% at 3.54 px, the same seam |
| Hook picture, 1080p, 4 s | No | About 1.8 px | 0.5%, no peak at the row size |
| Table's key, 1080p, 794 s | Yes | 2.43 px | 0.8%, strongest period 9.70 px, no peak at the row size |

- **Not measured at 4K for this film.** No 4K exists. The band is set in pixels (`fmn-render/src/stroke.rs:112`, `:921`, read in the local franken_manim checkout `5eacaaab`, not at the pin), so I expect a thinner seam about every 9.45 px, but that is a prediction.
- **Shared code:** the same helper paints the colour pictures in other chapters I did not review here.

**05-A comb.** `checks/comb_and_bars.py` mirrors `shade()` and `cell_input()`. `exp_plane` has 91 columns, so the centre column samples x = 0.0 exactly, where |e^z| rounds to just above or just below 1 and `shade()` jumps between band top and band bottom. It predicts 35 teeth in 183 rows. At x = 406 on frame 92 I find 35, and the prediction agrees on 183 of 183 rows. Other pictures also have odd widths (183 columns in 04 and zeta, 57 in the strip), but only e^z keeps its size exactly 1 down the whole centre column, and I saw no comb on them.

**05-F bars.** tan z tends to ±i far from the real line, so its size sits on a band jump and its angle sits on a phase jump. Hue goes flat, as the voice says, but brightness does not.

| Measure | y ≈ 2 | y ≈ 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Frame 200, luma of brightest over darkest bar | 1.94 (row 260, y ≈ 1.8) | 2.14 (row 200, y ≈ 2.8) |
| Formula as built, shade max/min | 2.09 | 2.15 |
| Size and phase bands both moved half a step | 1.08 | 1.01 |
| Only size bands moved half a step | 1.35 | 1.26 |

Moving the size bands half a step also clears the comb. Moving the phase bands takes the dark lines off the wheel's spokes, so the wheel legend would have to move with them. That choice is root's.

**05-D late result.** This does not depend on the scratch rate. `walk_and_count` is called from "where the colours run backwards" with a 2.5 s floor (`gallery.rs:261–290`, `:365–392`). That phrase is three words from the line's end, so the walk overruns the line, and the card appears only when the walk ends. 05_3 then fades it straight away. Frame 79 still reads "−2.0" at 30.5 s, frame 80 has the card at 30.7 s, and frame 82 has nearly lost it at 31.0 s. In 05_1 the same card is up from 13.5 s to about 16 s.

**05-J inset.** The box spans x −1 to 2 and y 0 to 3. The strip spans y 0 to 32. The leader runs from the box's top right, (2, 3), to the strip's bottom left, (−1, 0), which are not matching corners (`gallery.rs:691–695`). The only disclosure is the dim 18-point "strip squashed: 0 ≤ Im s ≤ 32". Nothing false is plotted: the four zero dots land where 32 units in 398 sheet px puts them, at sheet y 299, 214, 164, 97.

## What holds

**Colour against function.** `checks/colour_vs_function.py` re-evaluates each function with Python's `cmath`, plus my own Lanczos gamma and Euler–Maclaurin zeta, and compares hue on a 61 × 61 grid of frame pixels. The control uses the conjugate output, so the check can fail. WHEEL is copied from `model.rs:489`.

## Layout lint

The render's layout lint lists 15 entries: 2 in 02, 2 in 03, 6 in 04, 5 in 05. I checked each by eye and did not treat a clean lint result as acceptance of anything. Three are real and named above: "−1", "radius 0.25" and "2.718". "i" is also real, at 02_3 @31.8. "simple pole" at 79.4 is real, but for contrast, which the lint does not judge. The rest are text on opaque cards or legible over the picture. **Most of the defects in this report were not on its list**, including 02-B, 03-G, the 0.368 and 1.000 labels of 03-H, 04-A, 05-A, 05-D and 05-J.

## Teaching against the approved films

This comparison is bounded. I viewed single frames only, 7 in all: `raptorq_gf256` 1080p at 4 s and 794 s and 4K at 4 s (that one as a crop); `lie_robotics` 1080p at 150 s and 300 s; `hoeffding_d` v6.2 at 120 s and 260 s. I also viewed the two `release_v1` contact sheets. I did not read their reviews or see their motion. Since I wrote the chapters, discount my judgement of them.

- **Where these chapters fall short:**
  - Approved frames name what they draw. Hoeffding's plot has ticks 1–9 on both axes, and its formula uses the axes' colours. GF256's table names a × b, both ranges and an "answer" key. Compare 03-E, 04-B and 05-M.
  - Approved frames I saw hold nothing stale. Compare 04-H.
  - Lie's quiz card says "your turn", gives options, numbers its ring and says "pause for more time". Ours asks an open question with a bare ring. GF256 uses only a "?", so this is a choice and not a rule.
- **Where these chapters hold up:** these are my judgement.
  - The 03 grid map, with right angles carried to their images.
  - The 05 walker beside its output trace.
  - The tile zoom at the end of 05.
  - The picture-first reading in 05, whose carded labels (`05_gallery/frame-0080.png`) are the model for repairing 04-D.
