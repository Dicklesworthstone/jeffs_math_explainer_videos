# Reproducing the adaptive-grid experiment

The chapter that starts at 49:42.817 compares two grids on a flow with a known answer. The fluid is periodic in a unit square. Its vertical speed is 0.35, so the horizontal shear layer drifts upward. Viscosity spreads the layer. We know exactly how fast each Fourier mode decays. [How to Teach a Computer to Simulate a Fluid, local 4K60 cut](../../../narration_lab/third-wave-20261024/root/five-film-release-20261027/navier-production-20261120/owner-review27/navier_stokes_simulation_delivery_master.mp4) · [English captions](../../../narration_lab/third-wave-20261024/root/five-film-release-20261027/navier-production-20261120/owner-review27/navier_stokes_simulation_delivery_master.en.vtt) · [Native solver source](../../../tools/fluid_reference/periodic_shear.c)

Writing the horizontal velocity as \(U(y,t)\) gives

\[
\partial_t U + 0.35\,\partial_y U = 0.001\,\partial_y^2 U.
\]

The solver starts with a horizontal drift of 0.3 plus 18 Fourier modes. At time \(t\), a mode of wavenumber \(k=2\pi m\) moves with the layer and its amplitude is multiplied by \(\exp(-0.001 k^2t)\). This supplies the reference velocity at each grid point.

## Check pressure and time separately

For a square cell of width \(h\), the solver measures the flux left over after the pressure correction by computing

\[
\frac{\Delta t}{h}\left|(u^x_{\mathrm{right}}-u^x_{\mathrm{left}})+(u^y_{\mathrm{top}}-u^y_{\mathrm{bottom}})\right|.
\]

The four \(u\)'s are the velocity components normal to their faces, in the positive coordinate directions. The expression is the unbalanced volume over one step divided by cell volume, using unit depth. We measure it after the pressure correction and before any mesh change at that step. This exact periodic flow has constant pressure. The adaptive run at a budget of 10⁻⁹ reaches a maximum imbalance of 6.50 × 10⁻¹², already below the second budget of 10⁻¹¹. The tighter run reaches 7.32 × 10⁻¹⁴, and the largest sampled velocity-error fraction changes by 6.18 × 10⁻¹⁰. This tests the sampled error and the measured imbalance for this flow.
