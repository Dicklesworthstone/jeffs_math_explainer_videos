## Promise

Five trustees, one vault, any three open it. By the end the viewer can deal
three-of-five shares on an eleven-mark dial by hand and rebuild the secret
from any three. They can also explain, by counting, why any two shares fit
every secret equally well: eleven curves, one per secret, each from exactly
one of the 121 equally likely spin pairs. They know the limits too: fair,
secret spins with zero allowed, a prime number of marks, an honest dealer.
Two people can always guess. The promise is that their shares never improve
the guess.

## Vocabulary (one word per idea)

- **vault**, **door**, **alarm**: the thing opened. A wrong mark sets off the
  alarm, so guessing is a gamble, not a free search.
- **dial**: the vault's dial, 11 marks, 0 to 10. Arithmetic "on the dial"
  wraps past 10 to 0. **mark**: one of its positions. A number "lands on" a mark.
- **secret**: the mark that opens the door (5 throughout).
- **trustee**: one of five people, numbered 1 to 5. That number is also their
  **position** on the board.
- **threshold**: how many trustees it takes (3). Named at 01_5.
- **share**: whatever one trustee is handed. Named at 02_1. **piece**: only
  for the failed idea of chapter 02 (ordinary parts of the secret) and in
  Shamir's own words.
- **possible secrets** (the strip): eleven gold ticks 0 to 10 at the right
  of the vault, with a live count. It shows which secrets still fit what the person
  or group we are watching holds. It is computed from the model, never typed.
- **dealer**: the one who knows the secret and hands out shares. **spin**: a
  fair turn of a spare dial by the dealer.
- **line**, **curve**: what the dealer's recipe draws. "Curve" always means
  the two-spin kind. "Polynomial" is named once (09_7), after it has been shown.
- **helper** (curve) and **weight**: chapters 14 and 15, built on paper.
- **prime**: named at 13_6, after the ten-mark failure and the eleven-mark
  success.
- **partner** (of a mark): the mark that, times it, lands on 1. Chapter 18,
  after dividing has been done the long way in chapter 17.

## Continuing example (all chapters)

Dial of 11 marks. Secret 5. The dealer's spins are 7 and 7 (chosen for the
picture; every claim is checked for every spin pair). Trustee x's share is
5 + 7x + 7x² on the dial:

| trustee | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ordinary height | 19 | 47 | 89 | 145 | 215 |
| share (on the dial) | 8 | 3 | 1 | 2 | 6 |

The opening trio is 1, 2, 3 (shares 8, 3, 1). The pair who meet is 4 and 5
(2 and 6). The joiner of chapter 11 is trustee 1 (8). The second trio of
chapters 16 to 18 is 2, 4, 5 (3, 2, 6); chapter 17's other dealing of the
same secret, spins 0 and 1, gives them 9, 10, 8. The liar of chapter 22 is
trustee 3, handing in 2 instead of 1. Warm-ups: the two-trustee vault of A and B,
first with ordinary pieces 2 and 3 (chapter 02), then on the dial with 9 and 7
(chapter 03); and the two-of-five line with spin 4 (chapters 07 and 08).

## Color roles

- gold (`hero`): the secret, its column at position 0, the needle, the strip's ticks.
- blue: shares and trustees' points.
- teal: the opening trio when they act together; helpers' weights.
- purple: trustees 4 and 5, the pair who meet.
- yellow: the dealer's spins and the spin grid.
- faint: candidate lines or curves that fit but are not the dealer's.
- red: a leak, a struck-out secret, a wrong number, the ten-mark dial, the alarm.
- green: the door opening.

## Devices

- **The vault**: door, 11-mark dial with needle, alarm lamp.
- **The trustee rail**: the five numbered discs with their share cards. They
  stay along the bottom edge of every five-trustee scene, including the
  boards and plots. A trustee whose share is in use lights up; the others dim
  but stay in place. On boards the discs slide up onto their positions, so a
  trustee *becomes* a point and never vanishes.
- **The strip**: see the vocabulary. A caption under it ("for: trustee 1")
  names whose knowledge it is showing (outsider, one trustee, A, B, the
  pair); "wanted" captions show a goal, not a scheme. The ticks and the live
  count come from one model value (`View::possible` in `src/model.rs`).
- **The dial board**: columns at positions 0 to 5 (0 gold, labelled
  "secret"), rows 0 to 10.
- **The paper plot**: ordinary heights, before wrapping.
- **The spin grid**: 11 × 11 cells, first spin across, second up.
- **The padlock board**: ten locks labelled by pairs.
- **Times rows**: a strip of cells with k·x on the dial.
- **Cards**: lower-third "precisely:" cards and verbatim quote cards with
  their source. Think time uses the kit's 4 s gold ring with "pause for more
  time" after each ⟨predict⟩.
