## How to read

the shown precision, rounded the same way by voice and screen? Is every

Is every historical claim and quote sourced? At R2, also work through
`ruth_audit.md` (the mathematician audit, plan §5.3) line by line.

You may recompute numbers yourself (python3 -I, exact `fractions`/`math.comb`
where possible). If you do, say what you ran. A number you did not check is
